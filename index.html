About Me

I am a Quantitative & Financial Data Analyst based in London, bridging the gap between mathematical rigor, financial strategy, and modern data science.


With an academic background spanning a B.Sc. in Mathematics, an MBA in Finance, an M.Sc. in Data Science, and doctoral research (DBA) at Teesside University London, I specialize in transforming raw transactional and financial datasets into actionable commercial insights.


My core technical stack includes SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn), Tableau, and Power BI. Alongside my analytical projects, I serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant, guiding postgraduate students through data analytics frameworks and statistical decision-making.
