Welcome to My Blog: My Journey into AI, Data Science, and Finance

Published on August 6, 2026

Welcome! I'm delighted to have you here. This blog is where I'll share my journey of learning, building, and growing in the world of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Finance, and Technology. My name is Blessey Carmichael, and I have always been passionate about learning and solving real-world problems. My academic journey has taken me through Mathematics, Business, Finance, and Data Science, giving me a unique perspective on how technology can improve decision-making and create meaningful impact. Like many beginners, I started with questions, uncertainty, and plenty of trial and error. Every new skill, project, and challenge taught me something valuable. This blog is my way of documenting those lessons and making them easier for others to understand. Here, you'll find articles about: Data Science concepts explained in simple language Artificial Intelligence and its real-world applications Finance and FinTech insights Python, SQL, Power BI, and Tableau projects Career tips for aspiring data professionals Lessons from my own learning journey My goal is to make complex topics practical and accessible. Whether you're a student, a career changer, or simply curious about technology, I hope you'll find something here that helps you take your next step with confidence. This is only the beginning, and I'm excited to learn alongside you. Thank you for visiting, and I hope you'll join me as we explore the exciting world of AI, Data Science, and Finance together.
