This blog is where I'll share my journey of learning, building, and growing in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Finance, and Technology.

Who Am I?

My name is Blessey Carmichael. My academic journey has taken me through Mathematics, Business, Finance, and Data Science, giving me a unique perspective on how technology can improve decision-making and create meaningful impact.

Like many beginners, I started with questions, uncertainty, and plenty of trial and error. Every project and challenge has helped me grow.

Why I Started This Blog

I created this blog to document what I learn and explain complex topics in a way that anyone can understand.

My hope is that readers can learn from both my successes and my mistakes.

What You'll Find Here

  • Artificial Intelligence explained simply
  • Data Science tutorials
  • Python, SQL, Power BI and Tableau projects
  • Finance and FinTech insights
  • Career advice for aspiring data professionals
  • Personal learning experiences

My Mission

My goal is to make technical concepts practical, accessible, and useful for students, professionals, and anyone interested in technology.
